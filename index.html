Research

Paper submitted to IEEE CAFE 2026

¹H NMR chemometrics for honey authentication

iLabAfrica, Strathmore University, with ICube Laboratory, University of Strasbourg

I compare 400 MHz and 700 MHz ¹H NMR for telling honey bee and stingless bee honey apart. The pipeline uses PCA and PLS-DA, validated with repeated k-fold cross-validation, bootstrap and permutation testing.

I also study how spectral linewidth affects classification accuracy. That matters for labs that can't afford high-field instruments, which is most labs in the region.

In progress: graph RAG for querying scientific NMR data.
